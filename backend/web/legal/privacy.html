7. Retention and deletion

We retain account and operational data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legitimate operational, billing, and legal needs. To request account deletion or a privacy-related change, contact {{LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Account deletion removes Cloud keys, rooms, guest links, player records, and match history and removes the associated sign-in identity. Stripe may retain invoices, tax, payment, and dispute records as required for accounting and legal obligations. We may retain non-reversible keyed fingerprints of the email address and deleted sign-in identity to enforce prior-trial limits, prevent an existing session from recreating a deleted account, and, when an administrator selects an abuse-prevention block, prevent future Cloud access from that address. Unblocked deletion and trial fingerprints are normally retained for up to three years; abuse-prevention blocks remain until the operator removes them. Some security and dispute records may also be retained for legitimate operational or legal needs.
