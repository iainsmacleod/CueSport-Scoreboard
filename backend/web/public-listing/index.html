Who is CueSport Scoreboard Cloud for?

Whether you stream or record to your favorite platform, run a league or tournament and need player stats, or just want a history of games with friends — with or without a camera — Cloud is built for that.

  • Streamers and recorders who need to score from the table, not the PC
  • Organizers tracking standings and player performance
  • Players logging matches with no stream or OBS setup

What does CueSport Scoreboard Cloud do for you?

Used with CueSport Scoreboard*, Cloud lets you run the match from your phone so you’re not tied to the streaming or recording desk. Hand a focused guest link to a player or helper so they can score without account access. Every completed match feeds searchable history and leaderboards — useful for tournaments, leagues, or casual nights. With ad-hoc tables, you can score on the fly with no OBS dock at all and still keep the same Cloud history.

* Streaming tables use OBS with CueSport Scoreboard (the free OBS dock). Cloud is the hosted relay, remotes, billing, and impromptu seats for games you are not streaming.

A quick look at CueSport Scoreboard Cloud in action.

  • OBS Dock + Phone Control

    Keep the free OBS CueSport Scoreboard on stream, and run the same match from your phone over Cloud. Scorers stay mobile around the venue while viewers still see the overlay.

  • OBS CueSport Scoreboard Dock

    Run the match from the OBS dock, your phone, or a guest scorer’s device. Everyone sees the same score instantly, so your team can work from anywhere around the venue.

  • One Dock Key per Table

    Connect each table to Cloud with its own Dock Key. Start with one weekly stream and add more tables as your events grow, all managed from one account.

  • Ad-hoc Tables — No Stream Required

    Not every match needs OBS. Create a dockless seat from the dashboard and score league nights, side tables, and multi-table events that aren’t on stream — while your main table still runs on the dock.

    Same Cloud scoring, guest links, and match history as a streamed table. Your phone is the authority; helpers join with a scoring-only link. End or destroy the seat when you’re done and free the quota for the next game.

  • Score From Your Phone

    Move around the venue while controlling players, breakers, balls, fouls, racks, race details, and overall match state.

  • Guest Remote Links

    Hand a scorer a QR link with focused match controls, one active device per link, and no access to account or replay settings.

  • Multi-Table Control

    See every active OBS dock from one responsive dashboard, review the live score, and jump directly into the table that needs attention.

  • Cloud Match Statistics

    Keep a searchable record of completed matches, compare leaderboards, review recent results, and explore each player’s history from any device. You can also help viewers find your stream when you go live.
